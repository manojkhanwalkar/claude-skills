# Applicability, Thresholds and Dates

Status as of November 2026. Laws change: before relying on a date for a finding's severity, check the source listed (EUR-Lex, the Commission's pages, the regulator). Where a business fact decides applicability and it is unknown, record the assumption.

| Law | Applies when | Exemptions / thresholds | Key dates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GDPR** (Reg. 2016/679) | Processing personal data in the context of an EU/EEA establishment, or offering goods/services to, or monitoring the behaviour of, people in the EU/EEA (Art. 3). | None by size for core duties (Art. 30 records have a narrow <250-employee exception). | In force since 25 May 2018. The *Digital Omnibus* proposal (Nov 2025, procedure 2025/0360(COD)) to amend GDPR and cookie rules (new GDPR Art. 88a) is **still in negotiation** — Council mandate agreed 21 Oct 2026, Parliament position pending — so current law applies unchanged. |
| **UK GDPR + DPA 2018** (as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025) | UK establishment, or targeting/monitoring people in the UK. | As GDPR. | DUAA 2025 changes are commenced in stages (2025–2026): new recognised legitimate interests, relaxed rules on automated decisions with safeguards, some cookies exempt from consent (analytics with opt-out), PECR fines aligned to UK GDPR levels. |
| **ePrivacy Directive** 2002/58/EC Art. 5(3) (national laws: e.g. TTDSG/TDDDG in DE, CNIL rules in FR; UK PECR) | Storing or reading information on a user's device (cookies, local storage, SDK identifiers, pixels, fingerprinting) for anyone in the EU. | "Strictly necessary" for a service the user requested, or transmission of a communication. | In force. The ePrivacy *Regulation* proposal was withdrawn (2025). |
| **Digital Markets Act** (Reg. 2022/1925) | Duties fall on designated **gatekeepers** (Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, ByteDance, Meta, Microsoft, Booking) for their core platform services. Ordinary projects are **business users** that benefit (data portability, access, interoperability, alternative app stores/payments). | Only designated gatekeepers have obligations. | Obligations apply since March 2024 (Booking since Nov 2024). EC–EDPB joint guidelines on DMA/GDPR interplay consulted Oct 2025. |
| **Digital Services Act** (Reg. 2022/2065) | Intermediary services offered to EU users: mere conduit, caching, **hosting** (storing user-provided content), **online platforms** (hosting + public dissemination), **marketplaces**, VLOPs/VLOSEs (≥45 m EU monthly users). | Micro and small enterprises are exempt from most online-platform duties (Arts. 19, 29) unless VLOP. | Fully applicable since 17 Feb 2024. Art. 28 minors' guidelines adopted July 2025. |
| **AI Act** (Reg. 2024/1689, amended by the AI Omnibus Reg. 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026) | Providers placing AI systems/GPAI models on the EU market, and deployers using AI systems in the EU, or whose output is used in the EU. | Research, personal non-professional use, military. | Prohibited practices: 2 Feb 2025 (two further prohibitions from 2 Dec 2026). AI literacy (Art. 4): 2 Feb 2025. GPAI obligations: 2 Aug 2025. **Art. 50 transparency: 2 Aug 2026** (Art. 50(2) machine-readable marking: grace to 2 Dec 2026 for systems already on the market). **High-risk Annex III** (incl. creditworthiness/credit scoring of natural persons, employment, essential services, education): **2 Dec 2027**. High-risk Annex I (product safety): 2 Aug 2028. |
| **Data Act** (Reg. 2023/2854) | Makers of connected products and related services sold in the EU; data holders; providers of data processing (cloud/SaaS) services — switching and interoperability. | Micro/small enterprises exempt from some data-holder duties. | Applies since 12 Sep 2025; access-by-design for products placed on the market from 12 Sep 2026; switching charges abolished from 12 Jan 2027 (reduced until then). |
| **European Accessibility Act** (Dir. 2019/882; national laws, e.g. BFSG in DE) | Consumer-facing products and services in the EU, including **e-commerce, consumer banking and payment services**, e-books, electronic communications, passenger transport services' websites/apps/tickets. | Microenterprises (<10 staff and ≤ €2 m turnover or balance sheet) providing services. Disproportionate burden (documented). | Applies since 28 Jun 2025; transitional periods for existing products/contracts run to 2030 (check national law). Standard: EN 301 549 → WCAG 2.1 AA (aim for 2.2 AA). |
| **Cyber Resilience Act** (Reg. 2024/2847) | Manufacturers, importers and distributors of **products with digital elements** (hardware and software, including standalone software sold/licensed) placed on the EU market. Pure SaaS is generally out (NIS2 instead), unless it is a remote data processing solution of an in-scope product. | Non-commercial open source; products covered by sector rules. | Reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents (24 h early warning, 72 h notification, final report) via ENISA's Single Reporting Platform: **11 Sep 2026**. All other obligations: 11 Dec 2027. |
| **NIS2** (Dir. 2022/2555; national laws) | Medium and large entities in Annex I/II sectors (incl. digital infrastructure, ICT service management, digital providers such as marketplaces, search engines, social networks, cloud). | Small/micro mostly out, except certain sectors. | National laws from Oct 2024 (transposition ongoing in some states). |
| **DORA** (Reg. 2022/2554) | EU **financial entities** (banks, payment and e-money institutions, investment firms, insurers, crypto-asset service providers, credit rating agencies…) and their critical ICT third-party providers. | Proportionality for small entities. | Applies since 17 Jan 2025. |

## Similar laws outside the EU (see `global-privacy.md`)

| Law | Applies when | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CCPA/CPRA (California) | For-profit doing business in CA with > $25 m revenue (indexed; ~$26.6 m in 2025), or buying/selling/sharing PI of ≥ 100k consumers/households, or ≥ 50% revenue from selling/sharing PI. | CPPA regulations on ADMT, risk assessments and cybersecurity audits finalised 2025, phased from 2026–2030. |
| Other US state comprehensive privacy laws (~20: VA, CO, CT, UT, TX, OR, MT, IA, DE, NH, NJ, TN, MN, MD, IN, KY, RI, NE…) | Thresholds by number of state residents (often 100k, some lower; TX/NE have no numeric threshold for non-small businesses). | Common core: opt-out of targeted ads/sale/profiling, sensitive-data consent, universal opt-out signals (GPC) in many, DPIAs. |
| US state AI and ADMT laws | Colorado SB 26-189 (repeals and replaces SB 24-205, which never took effect): automated decision-making technology that materially influences consequential decisions, from 1 Jan 2027; CCPA ADMT regulations (above). | AG enforcement with a 60-day cure period; check the statute for the covered domains (financial services, housing, employment…). |
| COPPA (US) | Services directed to children under 13, or with actual knowledge. | Amended rule (2025): separate consent for third-party disclosure, data retention limits; compliance by Apr 2026. |
| GLBA / FCRA (US) | Financial institutions / consumer reports (credit). | Covered with security controls in `compliance-check`; FCRA adverse-action notices relevant to credit decisions. |
| LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA + Quebec Law 25 (Canada), DPDP Act 2023 + Rules 2025 (India), PIPL (China), APPI (Japan), PDPA (Singapore), Australia Privacy Act (2024 amendments) | Users in those countries. | Mostly GDPR-like; differences in `global-privacy.md`. |
| UK DMCC Act 2024 / Online Safety Act 2023 | UK firms with Strategic Market Status (DMCC); user-to-user and search services with UK users (OSA). | OSA illegal-content and children's-safety duties in force 2025 (age assurance for porn/harmful content). |
